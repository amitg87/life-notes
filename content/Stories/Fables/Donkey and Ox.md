Ox tells Donkey - I am too tired of my work in field - pulling the plow from morning until night. No matter how hot the day, or how tired my legs, or how the bow chafes my neck, still I must work. But you are a creature of leisure. Donkey is trapped with a colorful blanket and do nothing more than carry our master about where he wishes to go. When he goes nowhere you do rest and eat the green grass all the day.

 Donkey - (out of goodwill) - You do work very hard and I would help ease your lot. I will tell you how you may have a day of rest. In the morning when the slave comes to fetch you to the plow, lie upon the ground and bellow much that he may say you are sick and cannot work.

Next day the ox took the advice. The slave returned to farmer and told him the ox was sick and cannot pull the plow. Farmer said - hitch the ass to plow, plowing must go on.

Ox is given rest day and donkey is made to work. All that day the ass, who had only intended to help his friend, found himself compelled to do the ox’s task. When night came and he was released from the plow his heart was bitter and his legs weary and his neck sore where bow had chafed it.

Ox - You are good friend. Because of your advice, I have enjoyed a day of rest.

Ass - And I am like many other simple hearted one who starts to help a friend and ends up by doing his task for him. I did hear the master tell the slave to send for the butcher were you sick again. I wish he would, for you are a lazy fellow.

Thereafter they spoke to each other no more - this ended their friendship.
### Morale

If you desire to help thy friend, do so in a way that will not bring thy friend’s burdens upon thyself.
